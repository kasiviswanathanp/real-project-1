About the clinic

Care that puts movement
at the centre.

Anbu Physiotherapy Clinic provides physiotherapy and rehabilitation care under Dr. J. Jenani, BPT., MPT (Ortho), with a paediatric consultation service led by Dr. M. Jeyaraj, MBBS., DCH. Every plan is built around how a patient actually moves — assessed, guided and adjusted over the course of recovery.
